The menu

3 staples stay. The rest moves.

Three tried-and-tested regulars on every week. Everything underneath changes every fortnight, so you never have to read something the size of a telephone directory.

01

Staples

On every week

Never leave. Don't ask.

  • Say What!? Rib

    Full rack £15

    11-spice & herb dry-rubbed baby back ribs. Slow cooked until boredom kicks in, then hit with our cooked-out glaze.

  • Say What!? Fried Chicken

    £10

    3 whole boneless, skinless thighs. Pickle-brined for a straight 24 hours, rested in herbed buttermilk, coated in our spiced dredge and fried. Comeback Sauce or Tarragon Mustard Aioli. Flat-leaf parsley & chive.

  • Say What!? Fried Fish

    £10

    Whole white fish fillet. Spice-rubbed, flash pan-fried in dill butter, cooled, dipped through our sparkling beer & lemonade batter and deep fried. Golden Green Aioli or Tartare Verte.

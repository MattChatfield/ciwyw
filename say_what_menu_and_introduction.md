# SAY WHAT!?
### A CIWYW FOOD JOINT

A little history recap before we get to the present and ultimately future tell-it-on-the-mountain news.

Many of you will be aware that as wonderful as we can be, we can be just as useless.

Over the past five years we have attempted on a few occasions to launch dedicated takeaway ventures that did not last long, despite the public outcry and riots on the street.

We began during lockdown with a variety of week-to-week different menus. Then lockdown became "as you were" and the restaurant resumed its un-normal style of sit-down service.

Then came the "Take This Shit Home With You" menus, in which we served purpose-made boxes of different selections for 1, 2 or 4 people to enjoy in the comfort of their own homes.

Again, we liked the idea but not the actual execution of said idea.

"Come Get Some" was next. Right idea, wrong location type of operation, and we were starting to realise a pattern was occurring.

Fixed menus bored us.

Last summer came "Markdonalds", again a fixed-menu turnout based in Cowes with a drive-thru attached, done solely to try to wind up a multinational burger-slinging outfit who barely noticed us at all.

And so we sulked and allowed our typical pattern of "not happening quick enough" decision-making to take hold and put it on hold.

The business and demand were there, just not our attention or focus.

---

## SAY WHAT!?
A new eat-at-home takeaway and delivery thing from the kitchens of **Call It What You Want Travelling Medicine Show**.

Due to the sheer arsenal of dishes in our possession, from the restaurant days through to our more recent kitchen pop-ups across the UK and Europe, we've accumulated hundreds, if not thousands, of possible combinations of dishes, sauces, sides and other things we've invented along the way.

Rather than attempt to squeeze all of this into one top-heavy, overcrowded menu, we've decided to do things the CIWYW way.

**Three permanent staples. Everything else keeps moving.**

Our three tried, tested and much-loved regulars will be there every week. The ribs, our seasoned buttermilk fried chicken and our infamous fried fish.

Underneath those, the rest of the menu will change every fortnight, giving us the freedom to introduce different dishes, flavours and combinations without expecting you to navigate a menu resembling a telephone directory.

The rotating dishes will fall under two headings:

### HANDHELD DEVICES
Fairly self-explanatory. Food you can pick up and eat with your hands. Burgers, sandwiches, dogs and whatever else we decide deserves to be held between your fingers.

### CUTLERY & TOOTHPICK BIZ
Dishes requiring some form of cutlery to get them into one's mouth, and quite possibly a toothpick afterwards. Although we may supply the toothpicks ourselves.

---

And then there are **THE CARRIERS**. Our selection of sides and accompaniments, from seasoned fries and rice to mash and whatever else the kitchen has been playing around with.

The beauty of this approach is that we already have an enormous collection of recipes, spice blends, marinades, sauces and cooking methods to draw upon, with more being developed all the time.

The possibilities are rather overwhelming.

People never really knew what they were going to get when they came to eat with us during our five years operating within the confinements of a bricks-and-mortar restaurant.

The same sort of logic will apply here.

Always the staples. Always something different. Always that added CIWYW "oof".

And because not everyone wants to eat alone, we'll also be offering full cookout boxes for those who fancy throwing a dinner party, feeding the family or are just plain greedy.

So, to wrap this up.

A fixed menu is still rather alien to us. We are still unable to squeeze any fun out of one of those.

But this? Totally us.

You see, kids, there is a lesson here.

Work hard, keep following your dreams, mix that in with being poor, and wonderful things will occur.
